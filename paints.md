Upper walls	Cream
Lower walls (up to waist level)	Light green
Ceiling	Pure white
Doors	Dark grey

Blackboard wall	Light cream, with a dark green board
Description of the Classroom
A modern classroom is painted cream on the upper part of the walls and light green on the lower part. The ceiling is painted white to improve brightness. The doors are dark grey, while the window frames are white. The classroom has a dark green blackboard, neatly arranged desks and chairs, a teacher's table, educational charts and a notice board.

Visual Example

My advice: Use cream and light green as the main colours. They give the classroom a fresh, peaceful and modern appearance suitable for teaching and learning.
