1. Administrative Staff
These are responsible for the management and administration of the school.

Head Teacher/Principal: Provides overall leadership and supervision of the school.
Vice Principal/Deputy Head Teacher: Assists the head teacher in managing school activities.
School Secretary: Handles correspondence, records, and administrative duties.
Bursar/Accountant: Manages school finances and financial records.
2. Teaching Staff
These are responsible for teaching and facilitating learning.

Classroom teachers.
Subject teachers.
Nursery teachers (where applicable).
Special needs teachers.
Guidance and counselling teachers.
3. Non-Teaching Staff
These support the smooth running of the school.

Librarian.
Laboratory technician.
ICT/computer technician.
School nurse.
Sports and physical education instructor.
4. Support and Maintenance Staff
These ensure that the school environment is clean, safe, and conducive to learning.

Cleaners.
Security guards.
Gardeners.
Maintenance workers.
Cooks and kitchen assistants (where applicable).
5. Supervisory Staff
These help monitor teaching, learning, discipline, and school performance.

Discipline master/mistress.
Departmental heads.
Senior teachers.
Academic coordinators.
